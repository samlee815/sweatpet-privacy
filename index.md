# 汗堡包（Sweatpet / WorkoutBuddy）隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 7 月 10 日**

本隐私政策适用于「汗堡包」iPhone 应用及其配套的 Apple Watch 应用与表盘复杂功能（以下合称「本应用」）。本应用是一款运动养成应用：您的日常运动让像素宠物成长进化。

**个人信息处理者**：本应用由个人开发者 Yang Jin 开发和运营（以下称「我们」），联系方式：779182165@qq.com。

本政策说明本应用访问哪些数据、以何种方式收集、用于哪些用途、保存在哪里、保存多久，以及您如何行使您的权利。

## 一、我们访问的数据及收集方式

在您通过系统授权弹窗明确同意后，本应用通过 Apple HealthKit 访问以下数据：

- **读取**：活动能量（卡路里）、锻炼时间、心率、静息心率、心率变异性（HRV）。
- **写入**：您在 Apple Watch 上使用本应用主动记录的体能训练会写入健康 App，与系统运动记录保持一致。

**收集方式**：活动能量在您打开应用或进行锻炼时读取，实时心率仅在您用本应用记录锻炼的过程中读取；**静息心率与 HRV 通过 HealthKit 的后台更新机制收集**——当健康 App 中出现新样本时，系统会在本应用未处于前台时唤醒它读取该样本，以便压力状态与表盘显示保持最新。全部读取均以您的系统授权为前提，您撤销授权后后台读取随之停止。

此外，本应用会保存您在应用内**自行填写的心情记录**。

除上述内容外，本应用不收集任何其他个人信息（无账号注册、无设备标识符采集、无位置信息）。

## 二、数据用途

以上数据仅用于本应用的功能本身：

1. 根据活动能量和心情记录计算积分，驱动宠物的成长与进化；
2. 显示今日运动概况和活力值；
3. 基于您个人的 HRV / 静息心率基线估算压力状态；
4. 在您开启心率区间提醒后，锻炼过程中依据实时心率在 Apple Watch 上发出本地通知（提醒完全在设备本地生成，不经过任何服务器）。

## 三、数据的存储、流转与共享

- **所有数据仅保存在您自己的设备上**（iPhone 与 Apple Watch 的本地存储）。手表与 iPhone 之间通过 Apple 的 Watch Connectivity 框架在您的两台设备之间直接同步，不经过我们或任何第三方的服务器。
- **表盘复杂功能**：为了在表盘上显示宠物状态、今日运动概况和压力状态，手表应用会将一份数据快照写入设备本地的共享存储（App Group），由表盘复杂功能进程读取。该快照同样只存在于您自己的设备上，不会离开您的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch。
- **我们没有服务器**。任何数据（无论是健康数据、心情记录还是其他任何信息）都不会上传、传输或披露给我们或任何第三方。
- **不存在能接触您数据的第三方**：本应用不含账号系统、不含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK、不进行任何形式的用户追踪。
- **健康数据绝不会用于广告、营销或数据挖掘**，也绝不会出售、出租或转让给任何第三方（包括数据代理商）。

## 四、数据保留期限

全部数据保存在您的设备本地，**保留至您删除相应记录或删除本应用为止**。我们不在设备之外留存任何副本，因此不存在设备之外的保留期限。

## 五、您的权利

- **撤销授权**：您可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康」或「健康 App → 共享 → App 与服务」中撤销或调整本应用的健康数据授权，撤销后本应用（包括后台更新）即无法再读取相应数据。
- **查阅、复制、更正、删除**：本应用内展示基于您的记录计算的今日运动概况与宠物状态；已写入健康 App 的训练记录可在健康 App 中查阅、导出和删除。删除本应用即删除其保存的全部本地数据（包括全部心情与运动记录）；如需查阅、复制、更正或删除单条应用内记录，可通过文末方式联系我们获得操作指引。
- 如您在行使上述权利时需要协助，或对个人信息处理有任何疑问，可通过文末联系方式与我们联系，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

## 六、未成年人保护

本应用内容适合所有年龄段使用，不含账号系统，亦不会将任何个人信息传输至设备之外。我们不会有意收集未成年人的个人信息。若您是不满 14 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人的陪同和同意下使用本应用；监护人如对儿童使用本应用有任何疑问，可通过文末方式联系我们。

# Privacy Policy (English)

**Effective date: July 10, 2026**

This policy covers the Sweatpet (汗堡包 / WorkoutBuddy) iPhone app, its companion Apple Watch app, and its watch-face complication (together, "the app"). The app is a fitness companion in which your daily activity raises a pixel pet.

**Data controller**: the app is developed and operated by Yang Jin, an individual developer ("we"). Contact: 779182165@qq.com.

This is a translation of the Simplified Chinese policy above; in case of any discrepancy, the Simplified Chinese version prevails.

## Data we access and how it is collected

With your explicit permission via the system HealthKit prompt, the app **reads** active energy, exercise time, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability (HRV), and **writes** workouts you actively record with the app on Apple Watch to the Health app.

**How it is collected**: active energy is read when you open the app or record a workout; live heart rate is read only while you are recording a workout with the app. **Resting heart rate and HRV are collected via HealthKit background updates** — when a new sample appears in the Health app, the system may wake the app in the background to read it, so your stress state and watch face stay current. All reads require your system authorization; revoking it also stops background reads.

The app also stores mood entries you log inside the app. Beyond the above, the app collects no other personal information — no accounts, no device identifiers, no location data.

## How the data is used

Solely for in-app features: (1) computing points from active energy and mood entries to drive your pet's growth and evolution; (2) showing today's activity and vitality; (3) estimating a stress score against your own HRV / resting-heart-rate baseline; and (4) if you enable heart-rate zone alerts, delivering local notifications on Apple Watch during a workout based on your live heart rate (generated entirely on-device, never via any server).

## Storage and sharing

All data stays on your own devices (iPhone and Apple Watch), synced directly between them via Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. To render the watch-face complication, the watch app writes a snapshot (pet state, today's summary, stress state) to on-device shared storage (App Group) read by the complication process; that snapshot also never leaves your own devices. We operate no servers; **no data of any kind — health data, mood entries, or anything else — is ever uploaded, transmitted, or disclosed to us or to any third party**. No third party can access your data: the app has no accounts, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no tracking. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never sold, rented, or transferred to anyone, including data brokers.

## Data retention

All data is retained on your device only, **until you delete the relevant records or delete the app**. We keep no copies anywhere else, so no off-device retention period exists.

## Your rights

You can revoke Health access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, or in the Health app → Sharing → Apps & Services; after revocation the app (including background updates) can no longer read that data. The app displays today's activity summary and your pet's state, both computed from your records; workouts written to the Health app can be reviewed, exported, and deleted there. Deleting the app deletes all of its local data, including all mood and workout records. To access, copy, correct, or delete an individual in-app record, contact us at the address below for guidance; we will respond within 15 business days.

## Children's privacy

The app's content is suitable for all ages, has no account system, and transmits no personal information off your devices. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are under 14, please use the app with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian; guardians with questions may contact us below.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Changes take effect upon publication.
